Orchid

Orchid is a personal finance app that helps people understand their money and build a routine they can keep. Small, everyday actions help a garden grow, with Pip, a friendly gardening companion, along for the ride.

What it does

See your money clearly

Connect supported accounts through Plaid to see balances, recent transactions, spending, and net worth in one place. Connecting a bank is optional.

Save toward goals

Create savings goals, record contributions, and watch your progress. Orchid tracks progress; it never moves money between accounts.

Make small actions count

Check in, complete daily quests, and earn XP and virtual coins. Your actions help three plants grow in your garden.

Learn at your own pace

Short lessons on budgeting, emergency funds, savings goals, interest, and mindful spending, each with a quick quiz.
