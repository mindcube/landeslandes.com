About us

Landes Landes Innovation Inc. is a small, independent software company. We make consumer apps that help people build healthier money habits.

Our story

Scott Landes founded the company in Delaware in February 2025. Money is a source of stress for many people, and most finance apps add to it with dense charts and guilt. We wanted to build something warmer: an app that rewards small steps and makes coming back feel good.

That idea became Orchid, our first product. In Orchid, everyday actions, like checking a balance, finishing a short lesson, or saving toward a goal, help a garden grow.

What we care about

Respect for people's data

Financial data is personal. We collect only what our apps need, use trusted providers such as Plaid to connect accounts, and let people delete their accounts and data. Read the Orchid privacy policy.

Clear, honest products

Our apps help people track and learn. They are not banks, they do not move money, and they do not give investment advice. Prices and terms are shown before anyone pays.

Encouragement over pressure

We design for steady progress. Streak freezes, gentle reminders, and short lessons make it easy to pick up where you left off.
